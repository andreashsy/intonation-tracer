Semitones and Hz

Semitones (st) measure pitch the way we hear it: one semitone is one piano key. 12 st = 1 octave = double the Hz.

"typical" (0 st) is the speaker's typical pitch in this clip. Lines above it are higher, lines below are lower.

Why semitones? A man around 100 Hz and a woman around 200 Hz asking the same question both rise about 7 st, so their lines have the same shape even though the Hz are different. That's what lets you compare yourself with a model.

The voice range (Hz) decides which pitches are traced at all. Anything outside it is ignored, and the typical pitch is worked out from what's left. While you edit a range, its limits appear on the chart.

Hover over the trace, or touch and hold it, to read the exact Hz and st at any moment. Change Axis to see the chart in Hz or musical notes.
